# M-01: Overridden vault conversion logic makes inflation attacks more profitable

- Source: [Cantina public report](https://cantina.xyz/portfolio/e4d93441-0fe3-4b64-bf98-fa31ecef4fb5)

## Issue

The vault overrides share-conversion math in a way that weakens inflation-attack protection during low-liquidity and donation-skewed states. A seeded attacker can force later depositors into much worse share minting.

## Invariant

Vault conversion logic should preserve anti-inflation protection for later depositors, even after direct donations distort balances.
