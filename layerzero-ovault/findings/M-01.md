# M-01: Overridden vault conversion logic makes inflation attacks more profitable

## Summary

The vault overrides share-conversion behavior in a way that weakens inflation-attack resistance compared to safer virtual-share / virtual-asset designs.

As a result, an attacker can seed the vault, donate assets, and force later depositors into a much worse share-minting outcome than expected.

## Invariant

Vault conversion logic should preserve anti-inflation protection for later depositors, especially when the vault can receive direct donations before normal deposits continue.

## Root cause

The overridden conversion path does not preserve the stronger protection that safer virtual-balance style math would provide during low-liquidity initialization and donation-skewed states.

## Impact

An attacker can make the inflation attack more profitable and reduce or erase the victim's share mint on later deposit.

## Fix direction

Use safer conversion math with strong virtual-share / virtual-asset protection and verify that donation-skewed states cannot push later users into zero-share or near-zero-share outcomes.
