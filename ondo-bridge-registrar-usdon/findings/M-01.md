# M-01: Missing `BURNER_ROLE` breaks OFT outbound bridging

- Source: [Cantina public report](https://cantina.xyz/portfolio/91210482-130b-478a-848c-773029679a90)

## Issue

`BridgeRegistrar.register()` grants `MINTER_ROLE` to the OFT but does not grant `BURNER_ROLE`. Because the outbound OFT path burns local tokens inside `_debit(...)`, every send reverts until the role is granted manually.

## Invariant

The OFT must receive all required token roles.
